# Timeline mascot body — asset credit

The timeline uses `quaternius-body.glb`, a converted and embedded GLB of
Quaternius' **Universal Base Characters — Superhero Male FullBody** model.

- Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
- License: CC0 1.0 (free to use, modify, and redistribute)
- Runtime path: `public/models/quaternius-body.glb`
- Local changes: loaded with Three.js, the built-in face layers are hidden,
  and the existing LNMHACKS mascot face is layered on the rig.
